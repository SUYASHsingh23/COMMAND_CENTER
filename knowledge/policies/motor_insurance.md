# InsureAI Motor Insurance Policy Wordings

## 1. Preamble & Definitions
This Policy is a contract of insurance between InsureAI (hereinafter called the "Company") and the Insured. The Company will provide the insurance cover detailed in the Policy Schedule subject to the terms, conditions, exceptions, and limitations set out herein.

**1.1 Insured Declared Value (IDV):** The IDV of the vehicle will be deemed to be the 'Sum Insured' for the purpose of this Policy and is fixed on the basis of the manufacturer's listed selling price of the brand and model as the insured vehicle at the commencement of insurance/renewal and adjusted for depreciation.
**1.2 Own Damage (OD):** Damage to the insured vehicle due to accidental external means, fire, natural calamities, or malicious acts.
**1.3 Third Party (TP):** Any person other than the insured or the insurer, who suffers bodily injury, death, or property damage caused by the insured vehicle.
**1.4 Network Garage:** Authorized repair centers empaneled by the Company to provide cashless repair services.

## 3. Section I: Liability to Third Parties
The Company will indemnify the Insured in the event of an accident caused by or arising out of the use of the insured vehicle against all sums which the Insured shall become legally liable to pay in respect of:
**3.1 Death or Bodily Injury:** Death of or bodily injury to any person.
**3.2 Property Damage:** Damage to property other than property belonging to the Insured or held in trust or in the custody or control of the Insured.

## 4. Section II: Own Damage (Comprehensive Tiers Only)
The Company will indemnify the Insured against loss or damage to the vehicle insured and/or its accessories whilst thereon:
**4.1** By fire, explosion, self-ignition or lightning.
**4.2** By burglary, housebreaking, or theft.
**4.3** By riot and strike.
**4.4** By earthquake (fire and shock damage).
**4.5** By flood, typhoon, hurricane, storm, tempest, inundation, cyclone, hailstorm, frost.
**4.6** By accidental external means.
**4.7** By malicious act.
**4.8** By terrorist activity.
**4.9** Whilst in transit by road, rail, inland waterway, lift, elevator or air.

## 5. Add-On Covers (Included in Comprehensive Plus)
**5.1 Zero Depreciation:** The Company will not deduct any depreciation for replacement of parts in the event of a partial loss claim.
**5.2 Engine Protection:** Covers consequential damage to the internal child parts of the engine and/or gearbox arising out of water ingression or leakage of lubricating oil.
**5.3 Return to Invoice (RTI):** In case of a total loss or theft, the Company will pay the original ex-showroom price of the vehicle rather than the depreciated IDV.
**5.4 Consumables Cover:** Covers the cost of consumables like engine oil, gear box oil, lubricants, nut & bolt, screw, distilled water, grease, oil filter, bearing, washers, clip, break oil, air conditioner gas and items of similar nature replaced during an accidental repair.

## 6. General Exclusions
The Company shall not be liable under this Policy in respect of:
**6.1** Any accidental loss or damage and/or liability caused sustained or incurred outside the geographical area.
**6.2** Any claim arising whilst the vehicle is being driven by any person who does not hold a valid driving license.
**6.3** Any claim arising whilst the vehicle is being driven by any person under the influence of intoxicating liquor or drugs.
**6.4** Any accidental loss or damage and/or liability caused sustained or incurred whilst the vehicle is being used otherwise than in accordance with the 'Limitations as to Use' (e.g., private vehicle used for commercial purposes).
**6.5** Consequential loss, depreciation, wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, failures or breakages.
**6.6** Damage to tyres and tubes unless the vehicle is damaged at the same time.

## 7. Claims Procedure & Conditions
**7.1 Immediate Notice:** Notice shall be given in writing to the Company immediately upon the occurrence of any accidental loss or damage in the event of any claim.
**7.2 FIR Requirement:** In case of theft or criminal act which may be the subject of a claim, the Insured shall give immediate notice to the police and cooperate with the Company in securing the conviction of the offender.
**7.3 Surveyor Appointment:** The Company will appoint an authorized surveyor within 24-48 hours. The vehicle must not be repaired or dismantled without the surveyor's assessment.
**7.4 Cashless Facility:** Available at all Network Garages. The Insured is only liable to pay the compulsory deductible and depreciation (if applicable).

## 8. No Claim Bonus (NCB)
The Insured is entitled for a No Claim Bonus (NCB) on the Own Damage section of the policy, if no claim is made or pending during the preceding year(s). 
**8.1 NCB Grid:**
*   After 1 claim-free year: 20%
*   After 2 consecutive claim-free years: 25%
*   After 3 consecutive claim-free years: 35%
*   After 4 consecutive claim-free years: 45%
*   After 5 consecutive claim-free years: 50%
**8.2 Loss of NCB:** If a claim is made, the NCB drops to Zero at the next renewal.
