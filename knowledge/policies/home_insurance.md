# InsureAI Home Insurance Policy Wordings

## 1. Preamble & Definitions
This Policy is a contract of insurance between InsureAI (hereinafter called the "Company") and the Insured. The Company will provide the insurance cover detailed in the Policy Schedule subject to the terms, conditions, exceptions, and limitations set out herein.

**1.1 Building:** Means the structure of the home, constructed of brick, stone, or concrete and roofed with incombustible material, including outbuildings, boundary walls, and gates.
**1.2 Contents:** Means household goods, furniture, fixtures, fittings, electronics, and personal effects of the Insured and/or his family members normally residing with him.
**1.3 Reinstatement Value:** The cost of replacing or reinstating property of the same kind or type but not superior to or more extensive than the insured property when new.
**1.4 Market Value:** The replacement value of the property less proper allowance for age, wear and tear, and depreciation.

## 3. Section I: Fire and Special Perils
The Company will indemnify the Insured against destruction or damage to the Building and/or Contents by:
**3.1** Fire, excluding destruction or damage caused to the property insured by its own fermentation, natural heating or spontaneous combustion.
**3.2** Lightning.
**3.3** Explosion/Implosion.
**3.4** Aircraft Damage.
**3.5** Riot, Strike, and Malicious Damage.
**3.6** Storm, Cyclone, Typhoon, Tempest, Hurricane, Tornado, Flood and Inundation (STFI).
**3.7** Impact Damage by any rail/road vehicle or animal by direct contact.
**3.8** Subsidence and Landslide including Rock slide.
**3.9** Bursting and/or overflowing of Water Tanks, Apparatus and Pipes.
**3.10** Missile Testing operations.
**3.11** Leakage from Automatic Sprinkler Installations.
**3.12** Bush Fire.
**3.13** Earthquake (Fire and Shock).

## 4. Section II: Burglary & Housebreaking (Elite Tier Only)
The Company will indemnify the Insured for loss of or damage to Contents from the insured premises by:
**4.1 Burglary/Housebreaking:** Involving actual forceful and violent entry into or exit from the insured premises.
**4.2 Robbery:** Hold-up or robbery within the insured premises.
**4.3 Damage to Premises:** Damage to the premises resulting from burglary and/or housebreaking or any attempt thereat, up to 5% of the Sum Insured for Contents.

## 5. Section III: Additional Covers (Elite Tier Only)
**5.1 Rent for Alternative Accommodation:** If the insured premises are destroyed or damaged by any peril covered under Section I and become unfit for occupation, the Company will reimburse the additional rent paid for alternative accommodation.
**5.2 Public Liability:** The Company will indemnify the Insured against legal liability for accidental death or bodily injury to a third party or accidental damage to third party property occurring in or about the insured premises.

## 6. General Exclusions
The Company shall not be liable in respect of:
**6.1** Loss, destruction or damage caused by war, invasion, act of foreign enemy hostilities or war like operations.
**6.2** Loss, destruction or damage directly or indirectly caused to the property insured by ionizing radiations or contamination by radioactivity.
**6.3** Loss, destruction or damage caused to the insured property by pollution or contamination.
**6.4 Unoccupied Property:** Any loss or damage if the building insured or containing the insured property becomes unoccupied and so remains for a period of more than 30 consecutive days, unless prior approval is obtained from the Company.
**6.5 Willful Negligence:** Any loss or damage arising out of willful act or gross negligence of the Insured or his family members.
**6.6 Normal Wear and Tear:** Loss or damage due to normal wear and tear, gradual deterioration, or inherent vice.

## 7. Claims Procedure & Documentation
**7.1 Notification:** Upon the happening of any event giving rise to a claim, the Insured must immediately notify the Company.
**7.2 FIR Requirement:** In respect of loss or damage by burglary, housebreaking, theft, or malicious act, the Insured must lodge a First Information Report (FIR) with the police immediately.
**7.3 Documentation:** The Insured must submit a duly filled claim form, copy of the FIR (where applicable), fire brigade report (for fire claims), repair/replacement estimates, and proof of ownership/purchase for contents.
**7.4 Surveyor Assessment:** The Company will appoint an independent surveyor to assess the loss. The Insured must cooperate fully with the surveyor.
**7.5 Salvage:** The Company is entitled to take and keep possession of any damaged property and deal with the salvage in a reasonable manner.

## 8. Policy Conditions
**8.1 Underinsurance (Condition of Average):** If the property hereby insured shall at the breaking out of any fire or at the commencement of any destruction of or damage to the property by any other peril hereby insured against be collectively of greater value than the Sum Insured thereon, then the Insured shall be considered as being his own insurer for the difference and shall bear a rateable proportion of the loss accordingly.
**8.2 Alterations and Additions:** The Insured must notify the Company of any material alterations or additions to the property or its occupancy.
**8.3 Mid-Term Increases:** Sum Insured can be increased during the policy period by paying a pro-rata additional premium.
