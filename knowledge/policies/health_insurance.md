# InsureAI Health Insurance Policy Wordings

## 1. Preamble & Definitions
This Policy is a contract of insurance between InsureAI (hereinafter called the "Company") and the Policyholder. The Company will provide the insurance cover detailed in the Policy Schedule subject to the terms, conditions, exceptions, and limitations set out herein.

**1.1 Hospital:** Any institution established for in-patient care and day care treatment of illness and/or injuries that is registered with the local authorities and complies with all minimum criteria as per the Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2010.
**1.2 Network Provider:** Hospitals enlisted by the Company, TPA, or jointly by the Company and TPA to provide medical services to an insured by a cashless facility.
**1.3 Pre-existing Disease (PED):** Any condition, ailment, injury or disease that is/are diagnosed by a physician within 48 months prior to the effective date of the policy issued by the insurer.
**1.4 Day Care Centre:** Any institution established for day care treatment of illness and/or injuries or a medical setup with a hospital and which has been registered with the local authorities.

## 3. Waiting Periods
The Company shall not be liable to make any payment under the policy in connection with or in respect of the following:

**3.1 Initial Waiting Period:** 30 days for all illnesses (except accidents).
**3.2 Specific Illness Waiting Period:** 24 months for treatments such as Cataract, Hernia, Benign Prostatic Hypertrophy, Joint Replacement (other than due to accident), and Sinusitis.
**3.3 Pre-existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period:** 
*   **Health Shield Basic:** 48 months of continuous coverage.
*   **Health Shield Gold:** 24 months of continuous coverage.
*   **Health Shield Premium:** 12 months of continuous coverage.

## 4. Value Added Coverages & Riders
**4.1 OPD Cover:** Covered up to ₹10,000 per year (Available only in Health Shield Premium).
**4.2 Maternity Benefits:** Covered up to ₹50,000 for normal delivery and ₹75,000 for cesarean section, subject to a 24-month waiting period (Available only in Health Shield Premium).
**4.3 Annual Health Check-up:** Covered once per policy year for all adults, regardless of claim history (Available in Gold and Premium tiers).
**4.4 Cumulative Bonus (No Claim Bonus):** 10% increase in base Sum Insured per claim-free year, up to a maximum of 100% (Basic), 150% (Gold), and 200% (Premium).

## 5. Exclusions
The Company shall not be liable to make any payment under this policy in respect of any expenses incurred in connection with:
**5.1 Unproven Treatments:** Surgeries or treatments that are not scientifically recognized or unproven.
**5.2 Cosmetic Surgery:** Plastic surgery or cosmetic surgery unless necessary as a part of medically necessary treatment certified by the attending Medical Practitioner for reconstruction following an Accident, Cancer or Burns.
**5.3 Substance Abuse:** Treatment for alcoholism, drug or substance abuse, or any addictive condition and consequences thereof.
**5.4 Self-Inflicted Injury:** Intentional self-injury, suicide, or attempted suicide.

## 6. Claims Procedure
**6.1 Cashless Claims:** 
*   Pre-authorization required: Inform the Company at least 48 hours prior to planned admission, or within 24 hours of emergency admission.
*   Submit the pre-authorization form along with the health card and photo ID to the Network Provider's TPA desk.
*   Approval will be provided within 2 hours.

**6.2 Reimbursement Claims:**
*   Intimation: Notify the Company within 48 hours of admission.
*   Submission: Submit the original hospital bills, payment receipts, discharge summary, and investigation reports within 30 days of discharge.
*   Processing: Reimbursement claims will be settled within 15 working days from the receipt of complete documents.

## 7. Premium, Free-Look, and Cancellation
**7.1 Free-Look Period:** The insured has 15 days from the receipt of the policy document to review the terms and conditions. If cancelled within this period, a full refund is issued minus proportionate risk premium and stamp duty charges.
**7.2 Cancellation Grid (Post Free-Look):**
*   Up to 1 month: 75% refund of annual premium.
*   Up to 3 months: 50% refund.
*   Up to 6 months: 25% refund.
*   Exceeding 6 months: No refund.
*   No refund is applicable if a claim has been made during the policy year.
